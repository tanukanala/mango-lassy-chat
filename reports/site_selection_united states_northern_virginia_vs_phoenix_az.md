**Commercial Real Estate & Data Center Site Selection Report**

**Executive Summary**

This report compares the suitability of Northern Virginia and Phoenix, AZ as locations for data center development. We analyzed key metrics, including power availability and reliability, zoning and environmental suitability, and financial incentives and tax abatements. Our findings indicate that both locations have their strengths and weaknesses, but Phoenix, AZ stands out as a more favorable location for data center development.

**Detailed Location Analysis**

### Northern Virginia

**Pros:**

* High demand for electricity and a well-established power infrastructure
* Range of tax incentives and abatements for data center development
* Proximity to major markets and transportation hubs
* Access to a skilled workforce and a strong economy

**Cons/Risks:**

* Tightening zoning regulations and environmental sustainability concerns
* Limited power capacity and potential for reliability challenges
* Complexity of tax incentives and potential for abuse
* High energy consumption and potential environmental impacts
* Rapid growth in demand for electricity, which may lead to strain on the grid

### Phoenix, AZ

**Pros:**

* High demand for data center space in Phoenix, with the metro ranked fourth in total inventory among data centers in the United States
* Favorable tax incentives and abatements for data center development
* Growing share of renewable energy sources in the state's electricity generation
* Potential for 100% renewable energy in the state's power grid

**Cons/Risks:**

* Zoning regulations and land-use rules are not keeping pace with data center development, posing challenges for municipalities
* Environmental concerns, such as air quality and water quality, must be addressed
* High tax burdens for data centers, which can be a deterrent for investment
* Potential risks associated with the increasing share of data center energy demand, which could lead to economically unsustainable traditional cooling methods

**Final Recommendation/Investment Memo**

Based on our analysis, we recommend investing in Phoenix, AZ as the preferred location for data center development. While both locations have their strengths and weaknesses, Phoenix, AZ offers a more favorable combination of power availability, financial incentives, and environmental sustainability. The city's growing demand for data center space, favorable tax incentives, and potential for 100% renewable energy in the state's power grid make it an attractive location for data center development.

However, it is essential to carefully consider the zoning regulations and environmental concerns in Phoenix, AZ, and to work closely with local authorities to mitigate these risks. Additionally, investors should be prepared to address the potential challenges associated with the increasing share of data center energy demand.

In conclusion, Phoenix, AZ offers a more favorable location for data center development, but it is crucial to carefully evaluate the pros and cons of each location and to conduct thorough due diligence before making an investment decision.
