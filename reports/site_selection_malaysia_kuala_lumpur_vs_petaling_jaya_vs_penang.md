**Commercial Real Estate & Data Center Site Selection Report**

**Executive Summary**

This report provides a comprehensive analysis of three locations in Malaysia for data center infrastructure: Kuala Lumpur, Petaling Jaya, and Penang. We evaluate the power availability and reliability, zoning and environmental suitability, and financial incentives and tax abatements for each location. Our analysis reveals that Penang offers the most attractive features for data center development, with a high demand for capacity, government incentives, and a commitment to achieving net-zero emissions. However, all three locations have their pros and cons, and careful consideration is required for investment decisions.

**Detailed Location Analysis**

### Kuala Lumpur

**Pros:**

* Growing demand for data centers in Malaysia, driven by the country's digital economy and growing IT sector.
* Significant investment in data centers, indicating a strong commitment to the industry.
* Suitable regulatory environment, with clear zoning regulations and tax incentives.
* Opportunities for renewable energy integration, which could lead to a more sustainable power grid.

**Cons/Risks:**

* Growing power demand from data centers may pose challenges for power availability and reliability.
* Environmental considerations and compliance with zoning regulations may require additional planning and investment.
* The availability and terms of tax incentives may vary depending on the specific location and project requirements.
* The need for significant investment in data centers may pose challenges for project developers and investors.

### Petaling Jaya

**Pros:**

* Attractive financial incentives and tax abatements
* Suitable industrial or commercial land for data centers
* Relatively stable and available power supply
* Potential for green energy and renewable energy incentives

**Cons/Risks:**

* Lack of green energy and renewable energy incentives
* Potential for high load utilization and power reliability concerns
* Zoning regulations and environmental considerations may be a challenge
* Limited availability of suitable land for data centers

### Penang

**Pros:**

* High demand for data center capacity in Penang
* Government incentives for data center development, including tax breaks and property tax abatements
* Commitment to achieving net-zero emissions and increasing renewable energy capacity
* Potential for data centers to consume 40% of Peninsular Malaysia's present electricity consumption by 2035

**Cons/Risks:**

* Limited availability of green energy in Malaysia, which may impact data center sustainability
* Strict zoning and environmental regulations may limit data center development
* Potential risks associated with the rapid expansion of data centers, including power consumption and environmental impact
* Uncertainty around the long-term sustainability of data center incentives and tax abatements

**Final Recommendation/Investment Memo**

Based on our analysis, we recommend Penang as the most attractive location for data center development in Malaysia. The state's commitment to achieving net-zero emissions and increasing renewable energy capacity, combined with its high demand for data center capacity and government incentives, make it an attractive proposition for investors. However, careful consideration should be given to the potential risks and challenges associated with data center development, including limited availability of green energy and strict zoning and environmental regulations.

We suggest that investors consider the following:

* Conduct thorough due diligence on the location and project requirements
* Engage with local authorities and stakeholders to ensure compliance with zoning regulations and environmental considerations
* Explore opportunities for renewable energy integration and sustainability
* Monitor the long-term sustainability of data center incentives and tax abatements

By carefully evaluating the pros and cons of each location and considering the potential risks and challenges, investors can make informed decisions about data center development in Malaysia.
