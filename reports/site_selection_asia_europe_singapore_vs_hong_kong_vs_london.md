**Commercial Real Estate & Data Center Site Selection Report**

**Executive Summary**

This report provides a comprehensive analysis of three key locations for data center development in Asia/Europe: Singapore, Hong Kong, and London. Our expert sub-agent evaluations have assessed each location across four key metrics: Power Availability & Reliability, Zoning & Environmental Suitability, Financial Incentives & Tax Abatements, and Key Pros and Cons/Risks. Based on our analysis, we recommend Singapore as the top location for data center development, followed by Hong Kong and London.

**Detailed Location Analysis**

### Singapore

**Pros:**

* Robust and reliable power infrastructure
* Attractive financial incentives and tax abatements
* High rental rates for data centers
* Strong business-friendly environment and political stability
* Robust infrastructure and connectivity
* Growing demand for data centers in the Asia-Pacific region

**Cons/Risks:**

* Integration of renewable energy sources and grid congestion may pose challenges to power reliability
* Zoning regulations and environmental standards may create challenges for data center development
* Limited availability of land for data center development
* Potential for increased energy consumption and environmental impact
* Regulatory changes may impact the attractiveness of Singapore as a data center location

### Hong Kong

**Pros:**

* Highly robust and reliable world-class electricity power supply network
* Favorable zoning regulations and environmental considerations
* Strong financial incentives and tax abatements for data center development
* High demand for data center space, driven by growing technology and business needs
* Strategic location in Asia/Europe, with access to major markets and customers

**Cons/Risks:**

* High vacancy rate, which may impact demand and supply dynamics
* Regulatory challenges, including environmental reviews and zoning regulations
* Limited availability of land for data center development, particularly in densely populated areas
* Potential for increased competition from other data center locations in Asia/Europe
* Need for careful planning and coordination to ensure successful data center development and operation

### London

**Pros:**

* High demand growth rate for data center power
* Well-developed power infrastructure
* Various financial incentives and tax abatements available
* EU-wide energy regulations and environmental reviews may provide opportunities for innovation and sustainability
* London's data center market is expected to continue growing, driven by AI demand

**Cons/Risks:**

* Zoning restrictions and community concerns may pose challenges for data center development
* High power demand growth rate and increasing power requirements may pose challenges for the grid
* Environmental regulations and reviews may add complexity and cost to data center development
* Data center sales tax exemption was eliminated in 2026, but other tax breaks are available
* Targeted incentives, such as property tax abatements, may be difficult to secure

**Final Recommendation/Investment Memo Conclusion**

Based on our analysis, Singapore emerges as the top location for data center development, followed by Hong Kong and London. Singapore's robust power infrastructure, attractive financial incentives, and favorable business environment make it an attractive location for data center developers. However, the integration of renewable energy sources and grid congestion may pose challenges to power reliability, and regulatory changes may impact the attractiveness of the location.

Hong Kong offers a favorable environment for data center development, with strong power availability and reliability, favorable zoning and environmental considerations, and attractive financial incentives. However, the high vacancy rate, regulatory challenges, and limited land availability may pose challenges for data center development.

London's data center market is expected to continue growing, driven by AI demand, but the zoning restrictions, environmental regulations, and high power demand growth rate may pose challenges for data center development.

We recommend that investors consider Singapore as the top location for data center development, followed by Hong Kong and London. However, it is essential to conduct thorough due diligence and assess the specific requirements and challenges of each location before making an investment decision.
