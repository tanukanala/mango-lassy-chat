**Commercial Real Estate & Data Center Site Selection Report**

**Executive Summary**

This report provides a comprehensive analysis of three locations in the United States for data center infrastructure: San Jose, San Francisco, and Oakland. We evaluate each location based on power availability and reliability, zoning and environmental suitability, and financial incentives and tax abatements. Our analysis reveals that San Jose offers the most favorable conditions for data center development, followed by Oakland, and then San Francisco. We recommend San Jose as the top location for data center investment, citing its favorable power landscape, modified zoning rules, and tax incentives.

**Detailed Location Analysis**

### San Jose

**Pros:**

* Favorable power landscape with renewable energy and increased grid capacity
* Modified zoning rules to make data centers easier to build
* Uniform standards and best practices for data center projects
* Tax incentives, including sales tax exemptions and property tax abatements
* Personal property tax abatements of 75% of the tax due for 10 or 20 years

**Cons:**

* Strain on the grid due to increasing demand from data centers
* Need for more stringent regulations to ensure environmental sustainability
* Limited tax incentives compared to other locations
* Potential for community angst around health and environmental impacts
* Need for careful planning and management of power consumption to mitigate grid strain

### Oakland

**Pros:**

* Attractive financial incentives, including tax breaks and exemptions from various taxes
* Conditional use permit process allows for careful consideration of potential impacts
* City has implemented rules to mitigate environmental concerns
* Data center investment program provides additional support for data center development

**Cons:**

* High energy consumption and potential strain on the grid
* Potential environmental concerns, such as dust, noise, and glare
* Limited information on zoning regulations and environmental impact assessments
* Potential risks associated with large-scale data center operations, such as cybersecurity threats and data breaches

### San Francisco

**Pros:**

* High demand for data center space in San Francisco
* Growing power infrastructure to support data center development
* Moderate financial incentives and tax abatements
* Access to a highly skilled workforce
* Proximity to major tech companies and innovation hubs

**Cons:**

* Strict zoning and environmental regulations
* High power demand and potential grid capacity issues
* Complex tax incentive landscape
* Limited availability of land for data center development
* High costs associated with complying with regulations and obtaining necessary permits

**Final Recommendation / Investment Memo Conclusion**

Based on our analysis, we recommend San Jose as the top location for data center investment. The city's favorable power landscape, modified zoning rules, and tax incentives make it an attractive location for data center development. While Oakland offers attractive financial incentives and relatively favorable zoning and environmental regulations, its high energy consumption and potential environmental concerns make it a less desirable location. San Francisco's strict zoning and environmental regulations, high power demand, and complex tax incentive landscape make it a challenging location for data center development. We believe that San Jose offers the best balance of power availability, zoning, and financial incentives, making it the ideal location for data center investment.

**Recommendation:**

We recommend investing in San Jose, California, for data center infrastructure development. The city's favorable power landscape, modified zoning rules, and tax incentives make it an attractive location for data center development. We suggest working closely with local authorities to ensure that power consumption is managed effectively to mitigate grid strain and that environmental sustainability is prioritized.
